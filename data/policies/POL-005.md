# Fees and Service Complaints

Document: POL-005 | Version: 1.0

## Section 5.1
Fees complaints should provide fee amount and fee reference; Account Services investigates the application of fees.

## Section 5.2
Service complaints must describe the experience, date of contact and any previous contact reference if available.

## Section 5.3
Complaint handlers should distinguish customer allegations from verified records and record conflicting information explicitly.
