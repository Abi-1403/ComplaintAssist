# Complaints Intake and Triage

Document: POL-001 | Version: 1.0

## Section 1.1
Record the original complaint, a unique case ID, the reported impact and any previous contact.

## Section 1.2
Complaint priority must be reviewed by a handler. Cases alleging active fraud or immediate security risk require urgent specialist consideration.

## Section 1.3
Do not assume a complaint is substantiated solely because an automated classifier marked it high priority.
