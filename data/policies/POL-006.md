# Specialist Handover and Data Protection

Document: POL-006 | Version: 1.0

## Section 6.1
A specialist handover must include a factual summary, chronology, assigned team, evidence references, missing information and suggested next steps.

## Section 6.2
All AI-generated handovers are drafts. An authorised employee must review, edit if needed and explicitly approve before finalisation.

## Section 6.3
Do not insert sensitive personal information into external AI prompts. Reviewers must check evidence citations and retain a record of material edits.

## Section 6.4
If evidence is incomplete or the AI output is uncertain, mark the case for human review rather than manufacture missing facts.
