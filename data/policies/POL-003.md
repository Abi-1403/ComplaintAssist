# Fraud Escalation Procedure

Document: POL-003 | Version: 1.0

## Section 3.1
Suspected fraud complaints must identify available transaction details and record whether a customer security contact has occurred.

## Section 3.2
Cases alleging ongoing fraud or compromised credentials should be highlighted promptly for the Fraud team. The AI must not independently restrict accounts.

## Section 3.3
Specialist staff decide the investigation and any account controls. Drafts must not present suspicion as confirmed fraud.
