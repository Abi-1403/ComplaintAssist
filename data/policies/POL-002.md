# Disputed Payments Procedure

Document: POL-002 | Version: 1.0

## Section 2.1
For a disputed card payment, document the transaction date, transaction amount and transaction reference. Flag missing evidence in the handover.

## Section 2.2
Potential duplicate charges are referred to Payments. A specialist checks bank records before determining whether an adjustment is appropriate.

## Section 2.3
Do not promise a refund or assert that fraud occurred before investigation.
